# ChillarPay – Case Study

## Introduction

ChillarPay is a fictional UPI payments app in Jaipur, mainly used by college students and small shopkeepers.

It has 8 lakh users and receives approximately 2,000 customer support messages every day in English and Hinglish.

Its six-person support team cannot handle all messages quickly. Urgent fraud complaints sometimes get lost among simple customer questions.

## Business Problem

The Head of Customer Experience wants to use AI to:

1. Sort customer messages.
2. Set complaint priorities.
3. Draft customer replies.
4. Identify urgent fraud cases.

## Support Rules

Categories:
- Failed Payment
- Refund
- Fraud Alert
- Account & KYC

Priority:
- High: Money lost without customer's consent or account blocked.
- Medium: Money stuck, delayed payment or refund pending.
- Low: Information request or profile update.

Reply Rules:
- Use polite and short language.
- Use the customer's first name if given.
- Never promise a refund date.
- Reply in Hinglish if the customer writes in Hinglish.

## Objective

Compare zero-shot and few-shot prompting to find which method gives better customer-support results.
